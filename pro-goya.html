Goa Group Tour Package Overview

About the Tour:

A tranquil atmosphere, sparkling waters, unwinding ambiance, stellar nightlife, and an intimate setting make Goa a place for a perfect getaway. It is a place that offers a perfect blend of sun, sand, and sea; a place known for its sprawling beaches, world-famous nightlife, incredible cuisine, and historical attractions. From witnessing beautifully built age-old cathedrals, old Portuguese-style buildings to indulging in adventurous activities to feel the thrill within, Goa has a lot to offer to visitors.

From cruising through the waters of Mandovi River to hearing the mighty roar of white water cascading down over the boulders in Dudhsagar, this former Portuguese Territory has a charm that is hard to put into words. Being the coastal destination that it is, Goa has all to make sure that you have a memorable time amidst its beautiful beaches.

Quick Info:

  1. Route: Panjim - Mandovi River Cruise - Aguada Fort - Sinquerim Beach - Candolim Beach - Calangute Beach - Baga Beach - Anjuna Beach - Vagator Beach - Mangueshi Temple - Old Goa - Miramar Beach - Dona Paula - Panjim
  2. Duration: 5 Days \ 4 Nights
  3. Start Point: Goa Airport/ Railway Station
  4. End Point: Goa Airport/ Railway Station
