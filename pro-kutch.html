Kutch Group Tour Package Overview

About the Tour:

Located at the Indo-Pak Border, the Great Rann of Kutch or the world’s largest salt desert, the Great Rann of Kutch is a paradise for the holiday seekers. With this tour to this part of Gujarat, you will get to witness its vastness and also have a glimpse of how life thrives in the region. Conducted in two different batches; in December and January, the tour also opens several doors to the striking beauty of the salt desert and its cultures. Joining this tour in December, you can also participate in the ‘Rann Utsav’ and witness the colourful aspects of the Rann of Kutch. As the tour also revolves around some of the important landmarks and sites within the vicinity, you can also enjoy the grandiosity of the region.

Go sliding down the slopes of Solang Valley or shop your heart out at the local market, Kutch has it all. Explore the town of Kullu which is wonderfully blessed with a combination of adventure and nature. Finally, travel through India's Mini Israel (Kasol) to Manikaran, where you can cap off your mountain vacation with a relaxing dip in the hot water spring

Quick Info:

  1. Route: Delhi - Kutch - Solang Valley - Kullu - Manikaran - Kutch - Delhi
  2. Duration: 5 Days \ 4 Nights
  3. Start Point: Rajkot
  4. End Point: Rajkot
