Haridwar-Massoorie Tour Package Overview

About the Tour:

Indulge yourself in an excursion to the most famous hill stations in Uttarakhand - Haridwar, Rishikesh, Dehradun and Mussoorie.

Haridwar, known as 'The Gateway of the Ganga' is considered one of the most important pilgrimage sites for Hindus where one can witness the rituals for the famous Ganga aarti on the Ghats and take a holy dip in one of the most sacred ghats of India - 'Har ki Pauri'.

Quick Info:

  2. Duration: 5 Days \ 4 Nights
  3. Start Point:Delhi
  4. End Point: Delhi
